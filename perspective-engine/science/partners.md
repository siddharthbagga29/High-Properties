## 6. One-page collaboration brief (DRAFT, not sent; items in [brackets] are founder decisions)
**Perspective Engine: invitation to review and co-design a pilot study**

**What it is.** An interactive illustration for neurotypical colleagues of five attention and timing patterns that some people with ADHD report or show at group level: default-mode intrusions, reaction-time variability, delay aversion, salience capture and (off by default) hyperfocus. Each can be varied alone. It comes with a debrief, scenes that include adaptations and strengths, and voices written by neurodivergent advisors.

**What we do not claim.** No "feel what ADHD is like", no diagnosis or screening, no treatment or symptom claim, no statement about any individual. All numeric ranges are design values, not empirical estimates.

**Why we are asking.** The disability-simulation literature warns that short simulations can raise pity and discomfort without improving interaction attitudes (Nario-Redmond et al. 2017, N = 60 and 50), and VR empathy effects look stronger for emotional (d = 0.33) than cognitive empathy (d = 0.08). We want those risks tested by people who know the field, including the possibility that the product does not work.

**Proposed pilot (sketch, to be fixed in the protocol).** Pre-registered randomised comparison of the simulation-with-debrief against an informational control in a workplace sample; outcomes are interaction attitudes and a behavioural measure, with follow-up at 4-8 weeks; lived-experience advisors rate recognisability and acceptability to calibrate parameters.

**What we ask (any one is useful).**
1. Advisory review of the mechanism spec: your mechanism, our wording and ranges (about [2-3 hours]).
2. Co-design and ethics input: reciprocity (two-way scenes), framing, debrief, participatory methods.
3. Study partnership: pre-registration, recruitment, analysis, authorship.

**What we offer.** The open spec and parameters; pre-registration with a commitment to publish null or negative results; authorship by contribution; no use of your institution's name without written consent; [payment for advisors with lived experience]; [data-sharing and IP terms].

**Who and when.** [Founder name, role, contact]. [Funding route and timeline]. Reply with a yes/no and the person best placed to talk; a 30-minute call is enough to start.
