# Perspective Engine: Advisory Board Recruitment Plan (v0.1 draft, 2026-10-06)

Owner: Milton (ethics agent). Node V04. Input: codesign-charter.md v0.1. Status: draft for founder review. No advisor or organisation has been contacted; nothing here has been sent, signed or paid. Revised 2026-10-09 after audit: s3 and s4 now say a prototype exists (AF-ethics-1); the Orange County tiers are labelled as a proposal whose adoption is unconfirmed (AF-ethics-2).

## Summary (read this first)

Proposed pay: a floor of USD 50 per hour for every lived-experience advisor, for all time worked (screening, reading, meetings, async review, difficult-material labour), not contingent on approval. The floor is anchored to one US lived-experience benchmark (Orange County CoC, USD 50 "Advisor" tier, proposed in August 2025; adoption unconfirmed) and one UK one (NIHR, GBP 27.50). Both are snippet-level and neither is in the ND or commercial field, so the founder should set the final rate at or above the floor (founder gate). Recruitment runs through an open, accessible, paid-screening call via ND-led and ND-community channels, never through customers, prospects or cold messages. Section 4 holds a facilitator script for the first 90-minute session and a content-review template. The founder must clear every outreach, contract and payment.

Not used: salary-site averages for "advisory board member" (corporate director pay, wrong population). A US professional-consulting benchmark for ND advisors: **not sourced, unverified**.

**Proposal for the founder to decide.**
- Floor: USD 50/hour, the same for every lived-experience seat, so there is no hierarchy of whose experience is worth more. Treat it as a minimum, not a target. The charter promises "professional consulting level"; these benchmarks come from public-sector settings and probably understate what a for-profit company asking for a binding veto should pay. Ethics agent recommends the founder set the final rate above the floor and record the reasoning.
- Optional chair or co-facilitator seat: benchmark tier is USD 55/hour (Orange County "Leadership", a proposed tier, adoption unconfirmed). Proposal only.
- Researcher, clinician and buyer-side seats (charter s2 additional seats): paid at their own professional consulting rate. Figure unverified, founder sets.
- Paid time: screening conversation (even if not selected), onboarding and contract reading, preparation, live sessions, async review, written comments, check-ins, and time spent after a distressing item. Advisors log their own hours; we do not second-guess reasonable claims.
- Company-cancelled sessions inside 48 hours (proposal) are paid in full. Minimum 1 hour per booked session.
- Payment is never tied to approval, endorsement or tenure. A hold does not reduce pay.
- Expenses reimbursed on request: assistive technology, interpreters or support people, captioning, travel if any. Access costs are the company's budget, not deducted from advisor pay.
- Payment speed target: set by the founder in writing (suggest within 14 days; proposal, unverified). Pay method chosen by the advisor where feasible.
- Benefits and tax: some advisors may receive disability-related benefits where earnings can affect eligibility. Specific US and UK rules: **unverified, refer to counsel**. Offer paid time with a benefits counsellor, flexible hours or lower-hour formats, and never pressure anyone to accept pay in a form that harms them. Contractor classification and tax forms: counsel.
- No equity or revenue share (charter s2), to keep the veto uncontaminated by financial reason to approve. Separate non-conditional recognition is a founder option.

**Illustrative first-quarter budget (my assumptions, not evidence).** 6 advisors at about 15 paid hours each (0.5 screening, 1.5 onboarding, 2 charter review, 2 first session plus prep, 4.5 three content-review sessions, 4 async reviews, 0.5 check-in) plus 6 unsuccessful interviewees at 0.5 hour = 93 hours. At USD 50: USD 4,650. At USD 75: USD 6,975. Add access costs, payment-processing fees and about 20% contingency for extra sessions after holds (assumption). Excludes facilitator, note-taker and counsel fees.

## 2. Recruitment

**Target (from charter s2, proposal):** 5 to 7 advisors; people with ADHD the largest group; autistic advisors and advisors with co-occurring conditions; spread of age, gender, ethnicity, sector and support needs; at least one simulation-sceptic seat; additional (not substitute) seats for a buyer-side employee or manager and an ND researcher or clinician. Self-identification is enough. No diagnostic documents asked for or stored.

**Channels (all outreach is founder-gated; none contacted; fit and posting policies unverified).**

| Channel | Why | Risks and notes |
|---|---|---|
| ND-led and ND-community organisations (examples known to exist, fit not checked: Autistic Self Advocacy Network, ADDA, CHADD) | Trusted routes to ND adults; autistic-led groups suit the reciprocity questions | Ask permission to post; offer a fee or donation for the posting only if the org wants it, never as a substitute for advisor pay. CHADD's audience and ASAN's policies on commercial work: unverified. Some orgs will decline a for-profit; accept that. |
| University disability, neurodiversity and ND-researcher networks | Reach ND researchers and students | Students are a power-imbalance risk if a supervisor or course is involved; no recruiting via course staff. |
| ND professional communities (ND-focused professional groups, ND employee networks, ND consultancies) | Work-context experience; sector spread | Employee-network posts must not name or imply an employer endorsement. Keep buyer-side seat separate from lived-experience seats. |
| Public open call on our own site and social profiles | Self-selection by people who have read about us | Slower, skews to those with time and reach; pair with other channels. |
| Referral from seated advisors (after first seats are filled) | Reaches people outside standard channels | Referrals are invitations to apply, not appointments; same process; avoids one advisor's circle dominating. |
| Online communities (for example ADHD forums, subreddits) | Large reach | Check each community's rules first; many ban recruitment. Post only with moderator permission. Unverified for any given community. |
| Paid-panel platforms (for example Prolific, User Interviews) | Fast access | Built for one-off study participation, not a standing board; suitability and ND filtering **unverified**. Not recommended for core seats. |

**Do not recruit through:** our customers, prospects or buyer contacts' employees (coercion and conflict risk); clinicians' patient lists; cold direct messages to individuals; scraping social media; the founder's friends and family (veto independence); anyone who reports to someone with a commercial stake in the product.

**Process (proposal).**
1. Plain-language call (draft in s3), with the pay rate, the veto, the for-profit nature, the upsetting-material warning and the time estimate stated up front. Alternative formats: text, audio and large-print; apply by short form, audio, or video, with no cover letter and no CV required.
2. Short application: how you want to be contacted, access needs, relevant lived experience in your own words, any competing commercial interest, attribution preference. Optional demographic fields, never required.
3. Paid 30-minute conversation with a human facilitator, format chosen by the applicant (video, audio-only, text, async). Same questions for everyone; shared in advance.
4. Selection against the composition target by two people: the founder plus one person who is not the founder (an ND advisor once seated, or an independent ND reviewer, founder to appoint). Record criteria and reasons. Founder has no sole choice over who sits on the body that holds a veto over their work.
5. Tell unsuccessful applicants promptly and kindly, thank them, pay for the conversation, and ask consent to keep a waitlist.
6. Application data: collected only as listed, deleted from active systems after the round except waitlist consent (retention period unverified, counsel). Advisors, not staff, may later ask to see what is held about them.
7. Contract and onboarding pack before any work (founder-gated): plain-language agreement, charter, veto summary, pay terms, attribution and withdrawal terms, session script, named human contact and a backup.

**Board health signals (not product success):** share of review requests answered in the agreed window; holds raised and resolved (a board that never raises a hold in six months is a warning sign, not a success); advisor retention; advisors' own answers to a quarterly "is this working for you" check-in; hours paid versus hours worked.

## 3. Draft recruitment call (not sent; founder gate; advisors may amend)

> **Paid advisors wanted: help us decide whether and how a workplace simulation about ADHD should exist.** Perspective Engine is a for-profit company exploring educational scenarios about ADHD-related communication at work. A short browser prototype exists; no advisor or study participant has used it yet. We are looking for neurodivergent adults (ADHD, autistic, or co-occurring) to sit on a paid advisory board. Advisors can place a binding hold on anything we make about neurodivergent people, and we cannot override it. You will be paid at least USD 50 per hour for all time, including this first conversation, whether or not you join, and pay never depends on you agreeing with us. You may be asked to review material that depicts difficulty and can be upsetting; you can stop at any time and still be paid for work done. You decide how you are named, and can withdraw your name or words later. You do not need to share a diagnosis. We welcome people who doubt this idea should exist. Apply by form, audio or video, or ask for another format. Contact: [named human, founder to supply].

Check against charter s7: no experience-transfer, empathy, efficacy or endorsement claims. Do not add any.

## 4. Session scripts

Facilitator must be a named human (founder appoints; ND-affiliated where possible). Milton drafts; Milton does not run sessions or contact anyone. Note-taker is a second person, notes visible live and open to advisor correction. Not recorded by default; any recording needs each advisor's explicit, revocable consent.

**Before any session (T-5 days, proposal):** send agenda, charter, one-page plain summary, content note, participation options (video, audio only, text chat only, async written), the questions we will ask, pay confirmation, facilitator and backup contact names.

### A. Session 1: onboarding and charter review (90 minutes)

| Time | Step | Facilitator says or does |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Arrive and set up | "Camera is optional. Chat is always open and counts as speaking. Tell me or type if anything needs changing: lighting, pace, captions, breaks. Notes are on screen and you can correct them. This session is not recorded." Introduce roles. |
| 0:10 to 0:20 | Who we are and what we are asking | Plain description: for-profit; an educational simulation; a short browser prototype exists (`mvp/index.html`, wording drafted by the product agent); no advisor or study participant has used it yet. Say the evidence is mixed and simulations can raise pity and discomfort (Nario-Redmond 2017). "You may conclude it should not be made or used. That is a legitimate result and we want to hear it." No marketing language. No product claims. |
| 0:20 to 0:30 | How your role works | Walk through the veto (any single advisor can place a hold; staff cannot lift it; silence is not approval), pay not contingent, attribution options, right to withdraw name and words, emergency stop. "Questions? You can ask in chat." |
| 0:30 to 0:35 | Break | Camera off; return when ready. |
| 0:35 to 1:05 | Charter walk-through | Three sections at a time. Two minutes of silent writing or typing first. Then: "What is wrong or missing? What would make you walk away? What would you change?" Voluntary sharing, round-robin pass allowed. Facilitator does not defend the text. Record disagreement verbatim in the log. State that the charter takes effect only once advisors approve a version. |
| 1:05 to 1:15 | Working practices | Ask each advisor: preferred format (live, async, mixed), response window, notification channel, sensory and timing needs, how to flag an urgent concern. |
| 1:15 to 1:25 | Decisions and open items | Read back the charter open items (review windows, minimum group size, stop-rule thresholds, resource list, privacy). Ask which they want to decide first and what they need before a first content review. |
| 1:25 to 1:30 | Close and check-in | Recap amendments and holds. Confirm hours to log (session plus prep). Written follow-up within 2 working days (proposal). "If anything from today sits badly later, here is who to contact, and you will be paid for that conversation." Check in with each person by their chosen method. |

### B. Content-review session template (60 to 90 minutes, per release candidate)

1. Content note first, read aloud and in writing: what the item depicts, sensory intensity, duration. Offer to skip. "You can leave at any point and keep the full session fee."
2. Show the item with provenance: which advisor-sourced accounts it traces to and which scenario label states it is an analogue.
3. Silent review time (5 to 10 minutes), with sensory controls and pause on.
4. Prompts mapped to the charter harm table: Does it read as pity, comedy, inspiration or incompetence? Does it essentialise ("this is what ADHD is")? Are strengths, adaptation and variability present? Do both sides of the mismatch get to adjust? Does anything imply diagnosis or labelling? Does it match what you or people you know report?
5. Round-robin vote, pass allowed: **Approve**, **Approve with named changes**, or **Hold**. One hold stands until the holder accepts a change or a majority of advisors lifts it after hearing them. Facilitator does not argue for approval. No count of how many approved is shown before the holder speaks.
6. Log date, item, version, outcome, reasoning; read it back and let advisors correct it.
7. Ask each advisor: do you want your comment on the record, anonymous, or not recorded? Confirm hours and check in.

**Facilitator rules.** Never ask for diagnosis, treatment history or personal trauma. Never ask one advisor to speak for others. Never reward agreement or press for a decision to hit a launch date. Never pitch the product. Pause at any sign of distress: offer a break, an exit with full pay, and a human follow-up within one working day (proposal). Any claim near charter s7 goes to the ethics agent and advisors before it is spoken.
