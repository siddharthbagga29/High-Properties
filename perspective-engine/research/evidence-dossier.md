## 1. Disability-simulation evidence
- Nario-Redmond et al. 2017 (Rehabilitation Psychology), two experiments: simulations left participants feeling more confused, embarrassed and helpless, and more vulnerable to becoming disabled. Empathic concern rose, but attitudes about interacting did not improve, and in some cases got worse, with more discomfort and pity. Short simulations also ignore long-term coping and adaptation, so capability is underestimated. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28287757/ and https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2017/04/170411151019.htm
- ADHD-specific: only practice-based projects found (e.g., BeLEARN ADHD VR simulation, Lucid Reality Labs, a 2025 local "adults experience ADHD" event, and a 2026 caregiver VR study). I found no rigorous controlled trial showing ADHD simulations improve workplace behaviour or reduce stigma. A caregiver study noted refreshers are likely needed for lasting effects. https://belearn.swiss/en/research-practice/projects/adhd-vr-simulation/ and https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747563226001469
- Design implication: the Nario-Redmond critique applies directly. The product must avoid "walk a mile in my shoes" framing, include lived-experience voices, and stress adaptation and strengths.

## 2. VR empathy durability
- Herrera et al. 2018 (PLoS ONE): two studies, over 560 participants, with an 8-week follow-up. VR perspective-taking produced more petition signing for affordable housing than narrative text. Whether the empathy gain persisted was mixed (the NPT group, and the more immersive VR group, differed only on some measures); read the paper before citing "durable". https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6192572/
- Ventura et al. 2020 meta-analysis (Cyberpsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking 23(10):667-676; 7 articles, 9 samples): the published abstract reports a non-significant empathy effect, d+ = 0.21 (95% CI -0.37 to 0.79), and a significant perspective-taking effect, d+ = 0.51 (95% CI 0.15 to 0.88). An earlier manuscript version gives empathy d+ = 0.44 (CI 0.06-0.81); cite the published figure. Very few studies, so treat as weak. (Search-summary level; full text not read.) https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2019.0681 and https://producciocientifica.uv.es/documentos/5fb86fc729995207311cbd94
- Martingano, Herrera & Konrath 2021 meta-analysis (Technology, Mind, and Behavior; k = 43 studies, N = 5,644): VR improves emotional empathy (d = 0.33) but not cognitive empathy (d = 0.08). Its overall pooled empathy effect is unverified: an audit search summary gave SDM = 0.43 (CI 0.31-0.55), but my own searches did not surface that figure, so do not quote it until the paper is read. Cognitive empathy (understanding) is what a manager-training product needs, and this is the weak spot. https://tmb.apaopen.org/pub/vr-improves-emotional-empathy-only and https://scholarworks.indianapolis.iu.edu/items/7d7127c0-6c5e-4455-adea-d93c54ea48cc
- Net: the larger meta-analysis (Martingano, k = 43) finds no cognitive-empathy effect; the small one (Ventura, 9 samples) finds a perspective-taking effect with a wide CI and no significant empathy effect. Evidence that VR builds understanding (as opposed to feeling) is thin and mixed.

## 3. Double empathy problem
- Milton 2012 proposed that autistic/non-autistic misunderstandings are mutual and interactional, not a one-sided deficit. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/254245139_On_the_ontological_status_of_autism_The_'double_empathy_problem' ; ten-year review: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13623613221129123
- Crompton et al. 2020: information transfer between autistic peers was as effective as between non-autistic peers, and degraded in mixed groups. https://pure.qub.ac.uk/en/publications/autistic-peer-to-peer-information-transfer-is-highly-effective/
- Relevance: supports a "reciprocal" framing (both directions, mismatch not deficit), but DEP is an autism-specific theory. Applying it to ADHD is an extrapolation. A one-way "feel ADHD" simulation does not implement DEP; reciprocity needs paired, interactive scenarios co-designed with neurodivergent people.

## 4. ADHD neuroscience: defensible vs pseudo-quantitative
Defensible constructs a simulation can model (as experience analogues, not diagnostics):
- Default-mode interference hypothesis (Sonuga-Barke & Castellanos 2007): periodic DMN intrusions into task-positive processing; reduced DMN deactivation linked to RT variability. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17445893/ (doi:10.1016/j.neubiorev.2007.02.005) and https://sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0006899309004612
- Intra-individual reaction-time variability is among the most consistent ADHD findings, with some association to DAT1 genotype (candidate-gene findings are weak and contested). RTV: Kofler et al. 2013 meta-analysis of 319 studies, Clinical Psychology Review 33(6):795-811, greater RTV in children/adolescents (g = 0.76) and adults (g = 0.46) with ADHD, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2013.06.001 ; DAT1: Kebir et al. 2009 review (J Psychiatry Neurosci), RTV the most replicated marker for DAT1 10-repeat homozygosity but too few studies for a meta-analysis, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2647566 (both search-summary level)
- Delay aversion / temporal discounting linked with DMN hypoconnectivity in adolescents. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35032471
Not supported: "18 dB SNR" and "neurotypical filtering" are not established metrics. Any such number would be invented. Also, group-level findings do not describe an individual's experience, and ADHD is heterogeneous.

## 5. Prevalence and workplace programs
- Song et al. 2021 meta-analysis: persistent adult ADHD 2.58%, symptomatic 6.76%, both declining with age. https://www.jogh.org/documents/2021/jogh-11-04009.htm
- SAP, Microsoft, JPMorgan, EY, and Ford run neurodiversity hiring programs. SAP reported 215+ autistic employees and Microsoft 200+ hires through its program. Most are autism-focused hiring pipelines, not ADHD manager-training programs. Vendor claims such as "48% faster, 92% more productive" and ">90% retention" come from company or secondary sources and should be treated as marketing. https://hbr.org/2017/05/neurodiversity-as-a-competitive-advantage and https://autismspectrumnews.org/corporate-neurodiversity-hiring-programs-scratching-the-surface/

## 7. Comparables and cautionary tales
- Embodied Labs (caregiver/aging VR): $3.2M seed (Jan 2020), subscription licences to aging-services orgs; pricing not found publicly (unverified). Closest analogue; small funding scale. https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/vr-powered-caregiver-training-tool-embodied-labs-raises-32m-seed-round
- Akili/EndeavorRx: first FDA-authorised game-based ADHD digital therapeutic; 2023 revenue only $1.7M, struggled with payer coverage, pivoted to OTC, cut about half of staff and sold to Virtual Therapeutics for about $34M ($0.434/share) in 2024. Cautionary on reimbursement path. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1850266/000095017024023124/akli-20231231.htm and https://www.massdevice.com/virtual-therapeutics-acquires-akili/
- Talespin was acquired by Cornerstone (March 2024, now Cornerstone Immerse); Strivr scaled via 360-video at Walmart and Verizon (over 1M users); Mursion sells avatar-based soft-skills simulation. Exit/outcome details for 2025 not found. Equal Reality: not researched (unverified). https://www.axonpark.com/top-vr-education-companies-in-2025
- Existing ADHD-simulation offerings are small/project-scale (BeLEARN, Lucid Reality Labs), so the niche is open but unproven.

## 8. Clinical and reimbursement
- 0770T: Category III add-on "Virtual reality technology to assist therapy", effective 2023-01-01, practice-expense only, sunsets Jan 2028 unless converted or renewed. It only attaches to a primary therapy code, so it does not reimburse a standalone psychoeducation or training product. https://www.codingahead.com/cpt-code-0770t/ and https://abacodes.org/new-cpt-code-available-to-report-the-use-of-virtual-reality-technology-with-therapeutic-services/
- FDA: revised General Wellness guidance (2026-01-06) keeps enforcement discretion for low-risk general-wellness products, and FDCA 520(o)(1)(B) excludes some software for healthy lifestyle unrelated to disease. Educational/training content that makes no diagnostic or treatment claims is likely outside device regulation, but any claim to treat, diagnose or reduce symptoms of ADHD changes that. Get regulatory counsel to confirm. https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/01/fda-issues-revised-guidance-on-general-wellness-products and https://fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices

## 10. Free tooling for MVP
- three.js, A-Frame, and WebXR in the Quest browser: widely documented and free; not individually re-verified in this pass.
- WebGazer.js: typical error about 2 degrees (100-150 px) after calibration, up to about 4 degrees (175-210 px) in comparisons; sensitive to lighting, camera and head pose. Fine for coarse "where on screen" attention demos, not for fixation-level or clinical claims. https://cs.brown.edu/people/apapouts/papers/ijcai2016webgazer.pdf and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.06279

## Implications for strategy
- Drop quantum, "18 dB SNR", the 72% figure and the CPT bull case from any external material. They are unsupported and will cost credibility with scientific and clinical buyers.
- Build browser-first (WebXR optional); the enterprise headset channel just contracted (Meta commercial exit Feb 2026).
- Design against the Nario-Redmond critique: include lived-experience voices, show adaptation and strengths, and measure interaction attitudes and behaviour, not just empathy.
- Target cognitive-empathy and behaviour outcomes, where VR evidence is weakest; pre-register a small pilot with a delayed follow-up (4-8 weeks) to generate your own evidence.
- Frame reciprocity honestly: DEP is autism-specific; co-design with neurodivergent advisors and build two-way scenarios.
- Position as accessibility/inclusion-productivity training, not "DEI", given rebranding trends.
- Stay outside medical-device territory: educational/general-wellness claims only; no symptom, diagnosis or treatment claims. Confirm with counsel.
- Avoid reimbursement-dependent models; Akili's payer struggle and 0770T's narrow scope argue for B2B/university licensing.
- Use SBIR (NIMH) only with a clinical or research angle; budget realistically since Phase I is small relative to build cost.
- Validate price points ($25-60k) and TAM with 10-20 buyer interviews; the comparables (Embodied Labs at $3.2M seed) suggest a niche, not a $14.8B market.
