# Perspective Engine: LOI Template and Pilot Offer (V06, as of 2026-10-06)

## Summary (150 words max)
A one-page, non-binding letter of intent plus a short pilot offer. The buyer commits intent to a paid pilot for one manager cohort. The success metric is agreed before any data exists: the pre-registered day-30 AAI-verified score (share of 10 listed practices enacted and verified), intervention minus an information-module control, reported with its confidence interval whatever the direction, nulls included. The fee is not contingent on the result. The price anchor is USD 12,000 per cohort, an unsourced assumption (the financial model's pilot-price mode) to test in discovery; the memo's $25k-60k/yr range is unverified and not reused. Positioning is accessibility, manager effectiveness and retention, not DEI. All text in [BRACKETS] is a blank for the founder (checklist in Part C). Counsel must review before use. Nothing here has been sent; sending is a human-gated node (V09).

## Part A. Letter of Intent (one page, NON-BINDING)

**LETTER OF INTENT: Manager Accommodation-Practice Pilot**
Date: [DATE] | From: [BUYER_LEGAL_NAME] (the "Organisation") | To: [PE_LEGAL_NAME] ("Perspective Engine")

**1. Purpose.** The Organisation intends to run a paid pilot of Perspective Engine's browser-based practice module for manager working-style and accommodation conversations, with one cohort of [COHORT_N] managers in [BUSINESS_UNIT] (the "Cohort"). The module is an educational practice tool that Perspective Engine plans to co-design with paid ADHD advisors before the pilot starts ([CONFIRM AT SIGNING: advisors engaged and content approved; if not, keep this sentence as a plan]). It is not diagnostic, therapeutic, or legal-compliance advice, and no legal outcome is promised.

**2. Success metric, agreed now, before any data.**
- *Primary:* the intervention-minus-control difference in the mean **AAI-verified score at day 30** (window day 25-35). AAI-verified is the share (0 to 1, also reported as practices out of 10) of applicable practices from a fixed 10-item list of everyday management practices that a manager enacted in the prior 30 days and that are verified by a shared system or calendar log, or by a majority of at least 3 of the manager's direct reports in an anonymous survey. The control arm is an equal-length information module with the same commitment-planning step. Analysis is intention-to-treat with a linear mixed model (random intercept for team), as defined in the pre-registration (draft V03, science/preregistration.md, H1 and Statistical models 1; Schedule A: [DEFINITION_REF]). Verification source: [VERIFICATION_SOURCE].
- *Reporting:* Perspective Engine reports the estimated difference with its [95]% confidence interval, **whatever the direction, including null or negative results**. A wide interval will be reported as inconclusive, not as success.
- *Harm check:* the pre-registered harm monitoring (pre-registration H2: stigma, pity and fear, distress and manager self-efficacy items, before and after), reported alongside the primary result.
- *Lock:* the metric and its definition are frozen at pre-registration. Any change needs a written amendment from both parties before the first session.

**3. Fee (hypothesis, to be confirmed).** Anchor of USD 12,000 per cohort (assumption, unsourced; see Part B), final figure [PRICE_ANCHOR]. Payable [PAYMENT_TERMS]. **The fee does not depend on the result.**

**4. Intended commitments.** *Organisation:* a named sponsor ([SPONSOR_ROLE]); release of managers for one [SESSION_LENGTH] session; voluntary participation; access to the verification source in a form agreed with Legal. *Perspective Engine:* delivery, facilitation, instruments, and a written aggregate report within [REPORT_DAYS] days of day-30 close.

**5. Data and privacy (proposed, counsel to confirm).** Participation is voluntary. The Organisation receives aggregate results only; any group under 10 is suppressed. No diagnosis or health information is collected. No manager-level result is shared with the Organisation.

**6. Publication.** Perspective Engine may publish pooled, de-identified results, nulls included. The Organisation is named only with its written approval. The Organisation may review a draft for confidentiality for [REVIEW_DAYS] days but may not block or alter the results.

**7. Non-binding.** This letter states current intent only. Neither party is obliged to sign a pilot agreement, pay, or buy. Either may withdraw at any time without liability. No exclusivity arises. Only a definitive pilot agreement signed by both parties creates obligations. [OPTIONAL_CONFIDENTIALITY_CLAUSE_OR_DELETE]

Signed for Perspective Engine: [PE_SIGNATORY] ______________ Date ______
