# Perspective Engine: LOI Template and Pilot Offer (V06, as of 2026-10-06)

## Summary (150 words max)
A one-page, non-binding letter of intent plus a short pilot offer. The buyer commits intent to a paid pilot for one manager cohort. The success metric is agreed before any data exists: the pre-registered day-30 verified accommodation-practice outcome, reported as an effect estimate with its confidence interval, whatever the direction, nulls included. The fee is not contingent on the result. The price anchor is a hypothesis to test in discovery: every price in the financial model is an unsourced assumption, and the memo's $25k-60k/yr range is unverified and not reused. Positioning is accessibility, manager effectiveness and retention, not DEI. All text in [BRACKETS] is a blank for the founder (checklist in Part C). Counsel must review before use. Nothing here has been sent; sending is a human-gated node (V09).

## Part A. Letter of Intent (one page, NON-BINDING)

**LETTER OF INTENT: Manager Accommodation-Practice Pilot**
Date: [DATE] | From: [BUYER_LEGAL_NAME] (the "Organisation") | To: [PE_LEGAL_NAME] ("Perspective Engine")

**1. Purpose.** The Organisation intends to run a paid pilot of Perspective Engine's browser-based practice module for manager working-style and accommodation conversations, with one cohort of [COHORT_N] managers in [BUSINESS_UNIT] (the "Cohort"). The module is an educational practice tool co-designed with ADHD adults. It is not diagnostic, therapeutic, or legal-compliance advice, and no legal outcome is promised.

**2. Success metric, agreed now, before any data.**
- *Primary:* the difference between the intervention arm and a control arm (equivalent-content video) in the share of managers with at least one **verified accommodation action at day 30**, as defined in the pre-registration (Schedule A: [DEFINITION_REF]). Verification source: [VERIFICATION_SOURCE].
- *Reporting:* Perspective Engine reports the estimated difference with its [95]% confidence interval, **whatever the direction, including null or negative results**. A wide interval will be reported as inconclusive, not as success.
- *Harm check:* pity and perceived-competence items, before and after, reported alongside the primary result.
- *Lock:* the metric and its definition are frozen at pre-registration. Any change needs a written amendment from both parties before the first session.

**3. Fee (hypothesis, to be confirmed).** Anchor of [PRICE_ANCHOR] per cohort (see Part B). Payable [PAYMENT_TERMS]. **The fee does not depend on the result.**

**4. Intended commitments.** *Organisation:* a named sponsor ([SPONSOR_ROLE]); release of managers for one [SESSION_LENGTH] session; voluntary participation; access to the verification source in a form agreed with Legal. *Perspective Engine:* delivery, facilitation, instruments, and a written aggregate report within [REPORT_DAYS] days of day-30 close.

**5. Data and privacy (proposed, counsel to confirm).** Participation is voluntary. The Organisation receives aggregate results only; any group under 10 is suppressed. No diagnosis or health information is collected. No manager-level result is shared with the Organisation.

**6. Publication.** Perspective Engine may publish pooled, de-identified results, nulls included. The Organisation is named only with its written approval. The Organisation may review a draft for confidentiality for [REVIEW_DAYS] days but may not block or alter the results.

**7. Non-binding.** This letter states current intent only. Neither party is obliged to sign a pilot agreement, pay, or buy. Either may withdraw at any time without liability. No exclusivity arises. Only a definitive pilot agreement signed by both parties creates obligations. [OPTIONAL_CONFIDENTIALITY_CLAUSE_OR_DELETE]
