01 — Loss record

Five adjudicated outcomes. Five orders of magnitude.

Not projections. Court records and disclosed settlements, each cited. The spread between the smallest and largest is a factor of three million — which is exactly why carriers are excluding the risk instead of pricing it.

Below is what real companies actually paid when their AI failed. The smallest was $476. The largest was $1.5 billion.

Frequency is compounding

Stanford HAI’s AI Index recorded 233 AI incidents in 2024 and 362 in 2025 — roughly 55% annual growth. IBM’s 2026 breach study puts the average AI-involved breach at $5.9M against $4.99M without AI. Rising frequency against an unpriced severity tail is the definition of an uninsurable line, until someone measures it.

Failures get more common every year, and nobody knows what the worst one will cost. That is why we measure.
